3. Creating a New Article • Field-by-Field Guide Section 3

Click New Newsletter Article. You will see an easy-to-fill form. Here is what each box does:

1. Article Title Required

The headline for your update.
Example: "Quorum Court Adopts 2027 County Budget Priorities"

2. Publication Date Auto-Filled

Automatically fills with today's date and time. You can change this if you are backdating an earlier update.

3. Draft Mode (Checkbox) • Your Safety Switch Key Control
☑ CHECKED (Draft Mode ON)

100% Private. Keeps your article completely invisible to voters. Perfect when stepping away or working ahead.

☐ UNCHECKED (Ready for Public)

Ready to Publish. Article goes live on jpecke.com as soon as you click the green Publish button.

4. URL Slug Leave Blank

Leave this blank! The system automatically creates a clean web address from your headline.

5. Featured Photograph Recommended

Click Choose an image to pick or upload a photo from your computer or phone.

6. Caption / Alt Text Helpful

A short description of the photo.
Example: "Lisa Ecke presenting budget updates to District 6 constituents."

7. Article Summary / Excerpt Leave Blank

Leave this blank! The system automatically uses your first sentence for the preview card on the archive page.

8. Article Content • Main Story Area Required

This is where you write your story! It includes a visual formatting toolbar at the top with buttons for bold text, headings, bullet lists, quotes, and links (see Page 3).

5. Adding Photographs and Captions Section 5
📸 Main Featured Photo (Top of Article)

Appears prominently across the top of your article and on the main archive page:

  1. In Featured Photograph, click Choose an image.
  2. Click Upload new to pick a photo from your computer.
  3. Type a 1-sentence description in the Caption / Alt Text box.
🖼️ In-Body Photos (Inside Story Text)

You can place extra community pictures right inside your story paragraphs:

  1. Click inside your story where you want the picture.
  2. In the toolbar, click the + or Image icon.
  3. Select your image, enter a caption, and it inserts into your text!
✨
Photo Pro-Tip: Horizontal (landscape) photos look best for the top featured picture! Smartphone photos in .jpg or .png format upload smoothly without needing any manual resizing.

7. Saving Private Drafts Private

Need to step away or wait for more details before publishing?

  • 1
    Make sure Draft Mode is CHECKED.
  • 2
    Click the green Save button in the top bar.
  • 3
    Your work is safely stored under your Saved Drafts tab. It is 100% invisible to the public.
8. Publishing Live to Website Live

When your story is complete and ready for District 6 voters:

  • 1
    Make sure Draft Mode is UNCHECKED.
  • 2
    Click the green Publish button in top bar.
  • 3
    Click Publish now to confirm.
  • 4
    That's it! Live at jpecke.com/newsletter/ in 60–90 seconds.
9. Editing an Existing Article Section 9
  1. Go to jpecke.com/admin/ and log in.
  2. In the newsletter list, click on the article you wish to revise.
  3. Make your text revisions or change photos.
  4. Click Publish (or Save).
  5. The live article and archive update automatically!
10. Logging Out Safely Section 10
  1. Look at the top-right corner (or bottom-left sidebar).
  2. Click your user name / email address.
  3. Select Log Out from the dropdown menu.

Your work is always safely preserved upon clicking Save or Publish.

11. Frequently Asked Questions • Quick Help FAQ
❓ Do I need a GitHub account?
No! You only need your email address and password to log in at jpecke.com/admin/. Everything else happens behind the scenes.
⏱️ How long does publishing take?
Usually 60 to 90 seconds. Our automated system rebuilds the page and updates search indexing. Simply refresh jpecke.com/newsletter/ to see it live.
🛡️ Can I unpublish an article?
Yes! Open the article, check the Draft Mode box, and click Save/Publish. The article is instantly removed from the public website.
🔑 What if I forget my password?
Click Forgot password? on the login screen to receive a secure reset link by email, or contact your campaign webmaster.
