# Lisa Ecke's Newsletter Guide: How to Create & Publish Updates

Welcome, Lisa! This guide explains how to write, format, preview, and publish newsletter articles on your website ([jpecke.com](https://jpecke.com)) using the **Decap Content Management System (CMS)**. 

You do **not** need any coding knowledge or a GitHub account. Everything is managed through a clean visual editor in your web browser.

## 1. Logging In to the Editor

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox).
2. Go to: **[https://jpecke.com/admin/](https://jpecke.com/admin/)**
3. You will see the **Lisa Ecke Campaign &bull; Newsletter Administration** screen.
4. Enter the **Email Address** and **Password** set up for you during your invitation.
5. Click **Log In**.

> 💡 **Tip:** Bookmark `https://jpecke.com/admin/` on your browser bar so you can return to it anytime with a single click.

---

## 2. Tour of the Dashboard

Once logged in, you will see the main editor screen:
- **Left Sidebar:** Displays **Newsletters**. Clicking this shows your list of dispatches.
- **Top Tabs:**
  - **All:** Shows all newsletters.
  - **Published Articles:** Shows only the newsletters currently live on your site.
  - **Saved Drafts:** Shows works in progress that are not yet visible to the public.
- **Top Right Button:** **`New Newsletter Article`** (blue button) — click this whenever you're ready to write a new post!

---

## 3. Creating a New Newsletter Article

Click the **`New Newsletter Article`** button. You will see an easy-to-fill form with the following fields:

1. **Article Title:**  
   *The headline for your update (e.g., "Quorum Court Adopts 2027 County Budget Priorities").*
2. **Publication Date:**  
   *Defaults to today’s date and time. You can change this if you are backdating or scheduling.*
3. **Draft Mode (Checkbox):**  
   - **Checked (ON):** Keeps your work completely private as a draft. It will **not** appear on the public website.
   - **Unchecked (OFF):** Publishes the post to the public archive as soon as you save.
4. **URL Slug (Optional):**  
   *You can leave this blank! The system will automatically create a clean web address from your title.*
5. **Featured Photograph:**  
   *Click **Choose an image** to upload a photo from your computer (see Step 5 below).*
6. **Featured Image Caption / Alt Text:**  
   *A short description of the photo (e.g., "Lisa Ecke presenting budget priorities at the Quorum Court").*
7. **Article Summary / Excerpt:**  
   *1 to 2 sentences summarizing the article. This appears on the newsletter archive cards.*
8. **Article Content:**  
   *The main body of your update where you write your message.*

## 4. Formatting Your Text

The **Article Content** box includes a visual toolbar at the top:

| Button | What it Does | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B (Bold)** | Makes text bold for emphasis | **Important Deadline** |
| *I (Italic)* | Italicizes words or titles | *Washington County Quorum Court* |
| **H2** | Section Heading (large) | Section title |
| **H3** | Subheading (medium) | Subsection title |
| **Quote (`”`)** | Creates a featured callout blockquote | *"We must protect our taxpayers."* |
| **Bullet List (`•`)** | Creates a bulleted list | • Item one<br>• Item two |
| **Numbered List (`1.`)** | Creates a numbered step list | 1. Step one<br>2. Step two |
| **Link (`🔗`)** | Turns highlighted text into a clickable link | Highlight text, click the link icon, and paste the URL. |

## 5. Adding Photographs and Captions

### Main Featured Photo (At the top of the article)
In the **Featured Photograph** field:
1. Click **Choose an image**.
2. Click **Upload new** and select an image from your computer (`.jpg` or `.png`).
3. Enter a short caption in the **Featured Image Caption / Alt Text** field below it.

### Adding Photos Inside the Article Body
1. In the **Article Content** toolbar, click the **+ (Plus)** or **Image** icon.
2. Select your photo and enter a caption.
3. The image will be inserted directly into your text.

---

## 6. Previewing Your Article Before Publishing

As you type on the left, the **right-hand pane shows a live preview** of exactly how your newsletter will look to readers!

- The preview uses the official campaign font (**Cinzel** headings and **Inter** body text), official county navy blue and gold accents, drop caps, and photo styling.
- Check spelling, layout, and make sure your photos look crisp.

---

## 7. Saving Private Drafts

Need to step away or wait for more details before publishing?

1. Ensure the **Draft Mode** checkbox is **checked**.
2. Click the green **Save** button in the top right.
3. Your work is safely saved! It will appear in your **Saved Drafts** tab on the dashboard and will **never** be seen by the public until you're ready.

---

## 8. Publishing Your Article to the Website

When your article is complete and ready for readers:

1. Make sure the **Draft Mode** checkbox is **unchecked**.
2. Click the green **Publish** button in the top bar.
3. Confirm by clicking **Publish now**.
4. **That’s it!** The website will automatically build the new page and update the newsletter archive at [https://jpecke.com/newsletter/](https://jpecke.com/newsletter/) within 1–2 minutes.

---

## 9. Editing or Updating an Existing Newsletter

1. Go to `https://jpecke.com/admin/` and log in.
2. In the list of newsletters, click on the article you wish to edit.
3. Make your revisions, add new text, or replace photos.
4. Click **Publish** (or **Save**).
5. The live article and archive will update automatically.

---

## 10. Logging Out

When you are finished:
1. Look at the top right of the screen (or the bottom of the left sidebar) where your name or email appears.
2. Click your user icon / email and select **Log Out**.

---

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions & Support

- **Do I need a GitHub account?**  
  *No! You only need the email and password you used to log in at `jpecke.com/admin/`.*
- **How long does it take for a published post to appear online?**  
  *Usually 60 to 90 seconds after clicking Publish. Simply refresh `https://jpecke.com/newsletter/` in your browser.*
- **Can I unpublish an article if I made a mistake?**  
  *Yes. Open the article, check the **Draft Mode** box, and click Save/Publish. The article will immediately be removed from the public website.*
- **Who do I contact if I forget my password?**  
  *Click the "Forgot password?" link on the login screen, or reach out to your website administrator.*
