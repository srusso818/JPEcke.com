As your Justice of the Peace representing District 6, one of my highest responsibilities is serving on the **Quorum Court Finance and Personnel Committees**. Every dollar allocated in our county budget belongs to the hardworking taxpayers of Washington County, and my commitment is to ensure government lives strictly within its means while funding core constitutional obligations.

During our recent committee work sessions, we focused on several key priorities to keep Washington County fiscally strong, secure, and ready for future growth.

## Core Budget Highlights & Conservative Stewardship

Our approach this session was defined by fiscal discipline, prioritizing needs over wants, and refusing to expand bureaucratic programs:

* **Balanced, Zero-Debt Budgeting:** Washington County remains in solid financial standing because we resist unchecked budget expansions and maintain robust emergency reserve funds.
* **Prioritizing Law Enforcement:** We allocated dedicated resources to modernizing communication gear and patrol vehicles for the Washington County Sheriff’s Office.
* **Detention Center Staffing & Safety:** Ensuring our detention deputies receive competitive compensation to reduce turnover and maintain a safe facility.
* **Essential County Road & Bridge Maintenance:** Directing infrastructure dollars to high-traffic county arteries and bridge safety inspections throughout District 6.

> "True fiscal conservatism isn't just about spending less; it is about spending wisely, holding every department accountable to the taxpayers, and guaranteeing that vital safety services are never compromised." — Lisa Ecke

## Ensuring Accountability in Every Department

During our hearings, each department head presented detailed operational audits. We examined line items carefully to eliminate duplicate contracts and streamline procurement. By leveraging technology in county offices, we are able to maintain high standards of public service without unnecessary headcounts.

### What Lies Ahead for the Quorum Court

Over the next month, the full Quorum Court will review the final budget ordinance. I encourage District 6 residents to reach out with questions, suggestions, or concerns. Your input guides my votes on the court every single day.

Thank you for the privilege of serving as your Justice of the Peace. Together, we are keeping Washington County safe, prosperous, and fiscally responsible.
