Serving District 6: Community Projects and Quorum Court Swearing-In

Lisa Ecke Justice of the Peace, District 6
• October 1, 2026 • 2 min read
Reflecting on recent community service milestones across Springdale and key resolutions passed in the Quorum Court to support local county roads and families.

Serving the residents of District 6 in Washington County continues to be one of the greatest honors of my life. Since taking the oath of office alongside County Judge Heath Ward and our fellow Quorum Court members, my mission has remained steady: listen to our neighbors, protect constitutional rights, and deliver practical results.

Local government is at its best when elected officials stay engaged directly with the families, business owners, and faith leaders they represent.

Recent Community Outreach & Hands-On Service

Over the past few weeks, I’ve had the opportunity to participate in several impactful initiatives across Springdale and Northwest Arkansas:

  1. Food Security & Pantry Partnerships: Working alongside local volunteer teams to ensure our local pantries are stocked heading into the winter months.
  2. First Responder Appreciation: Meeting with local volunteer fire departments and county deputies to discuss rural emergency response equipment needs.
  3. Youth & Civics Engagement: Speaking with local high school students about county government, civic duty, and the constitutional duties of Justices of the Peace.

"Public service is about showing up where people are hurting, where projects need an extra set of hands, and where common sense can solve everyday problems."

Legislative Action in the Quorum Court

At our recent general session, the court advanced key ordinances focused on:

  • Expanding Clean Drainage Projects: Addressing heavy rainfall runoff issues affecting rural properties and county roadways in eastern Washington County.
  • Modernizing Emergency Response Radio Infrastructure: Ensuring interoperability between county dispatch and municipal police and fire units.
  • Protecting Private Property Rights: Voting against burdensome land-use restrictions that infringe upon landowner rights.

If you have an idea for our district or need assistance navigating county services, please never hesitate to contact me directly.

About Justice of the Peace Lisa Ecke

Proven Leadership for Washington County

Lisa Ecke represents District 6 on the Washington County Quorum Court, serving on the Personnel, Finance, and Jail Committees. A dedicated advocate for responsible fiscal stewardship, law enforcement support, and family values in Northwest Arkansas.
